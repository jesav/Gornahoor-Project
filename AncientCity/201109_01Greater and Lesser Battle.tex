\section{Greater and Lesser Battle}

Just to give readers something to live for today.

\begin{quotex}
The battle differentiates, distinguishes, and creates hierarchy, especially when --- to use some traditional expressions --- it is not the small battle, but the great battle. It is not the battle of man against man or against the environment, but the battle of the supernatural element of a man against everything that within him is nature, sensation, materiality, anxiety, turmoil, a mirage of vain greatness.

\emph{Against the chaos and the anti-race that are within him, before they are outside of him.}
\end{quotex}
