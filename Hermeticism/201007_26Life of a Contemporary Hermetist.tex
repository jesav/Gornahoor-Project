\section{Life of a Contemporary Hermetist}

A Hermetist is comfortable whether

\begin{itemize}
\item performing in obscurity the activities of so-called ordinary life,
\item or in the company of the high and mighty,
\item or alone with his studies, inscrutable to the most intimate of his friends, relatives, and lovers,
\item or entranced by the seductions of courtly love,
\item or in prayerful contemplation in the presence of beautiful old men.
\end{itemize}
