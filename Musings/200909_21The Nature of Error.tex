\section{The Nature of Error}

\begin{quotex}
The nature of error is not to know itself as error, whereas the nature of truth is to posit itself as consciousness of truth, while knowing at the same time error as error.
\flright{\textsc{Julius Evola}, \textit{Pagan Imperialism}}
\end{quotex}
