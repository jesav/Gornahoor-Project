\section{To Become a Man}

\begin{quotex}
To become a \emph{man} at once is impossible. One has to mould oneself into a man. Here discipline is required. But it is precisely this relentless self-discipline that our present-day thinkers reject. Now it is in the relentless self-discipline and uninterrupted work on one's self that our citizen could reveal himself.
\flright{\textsc{Dostoievsky}, \emph{from Diary of a Writer}}
\end{quotex}
