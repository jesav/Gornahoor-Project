\section{Factory Authorized Service Centers}

Mr. X was driving in a remote area in the southwest. He stopped to car to heed the call of nature, but when he returned, the car would not start. Nightfall is approaching, he forgot to bring a blanket, and in the back seat, he noticed there were no more bottles of water. He reported it to AAA, but they won't be able to get to him for at least three hours. He evaluated his choices:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\Alph{enumi}.}

\item His owner's manual advises him to go to a factory authorized service center, approved by the manufacturer's headquarters, where he can get genuine parts. The nearest one won't open until Monday morning.

\item He does a search on google for service stations. He reads all the reviews on line and picks the one with the most stars.

\item He calls his mother who has a friend who knows someone whose husband flies a helicopter. Perhaps he can be air-lifted out.

\item He gets the tire iron out of his trunk, and gives his starter several hard whacks. He then is able to start his engine and continue on to his destination.
\end{enumerate}

What should Mr X do?

\begin{itemize}

\item A. It is important not to void his warranty.

\item B. A thousand people cannot be wrong.

\item C. If you think positive thoughts, the universe will meet your needs.

\item D. This is absurd and I will report you to the authorities.

\item None of the Above. I sense there is a symbolic dimension to all this, but it eludes me.
\end{itemize}
