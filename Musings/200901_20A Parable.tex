\section{A Parable}

\begin{quotex}
Not every end is a goal. The end of a melody is not its goal; but nonetheless, if the melody had not reached its end, it would not have reached its goal. A parable.
\flright{\textbf{Friedrich Nietzsche}}
\end{quotex}
