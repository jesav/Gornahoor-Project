\section{The Sacred Syllables of Auguste Comte}

\begin{itemize}\item Order and Progress

\item Family, Country, Humanity

\item Love as the principle and order as the basis, progress as the goal

\item Everything is relative, that is the absolute principle

\item Infer in order to deduce, so as to construct

\item To know in order to foresee, to foresee in order to make possible

\item The spirit must always be the master of the heart, never its slave

\item Progress is the development of order

\item Subordination is the basis of improvement

\item The most noble phenomena are everywhere subordinated to the coarsest

\item The living will always, and more and more, be governed by the dead

\item Man must more and more subordinate himself to humanity
\end{itemize}
