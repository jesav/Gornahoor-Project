\section{Seed of the Father}

When I was a young boy, my father would tell me once or twice about women he had known prior to my mother, and even how he almost married them. It was a curious idea for me and I would speculate how my life would have been with a different mother. Never once did I doubt the possibility of my own existence.

Childhood ignorance or pure insight?
